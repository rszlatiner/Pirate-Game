🧑‍🏫 For teachers: using this in a creative writing class
  • Branching narrative: have students map the story on paper. Which choices lead to which of the 10 endings? Where do paths merge? Which characters are needed to unlock the best ending?
  • Captain's Log prompts appear at five points (similes, dialogue, puns, monologue, voice). Students save their writing and copy their whole log into a document at the end.
  • Characters with jobs: every side character is essential to the plot. Ask: what does each character want, and what would change if they were removed?
  • Humour toolkit: look for exaggeration, deadpan, puns, running gags (the trousers, Kevin, the monocle) and rule-of-three.
  • Leaderboard: scores are stored on each device. For a class competition, use the "Copy leaderboard" button and collect results, or have students compare "Journeys" and discuss which choices earned the most doubloons.
  • Vocabulary round (Grades 8–9): the Captain's Word Test on the deck asks five questions drawn from 22 Tier-2 words (e.g. ominous, ambiguous, meticulous, ubiquitous): fill-in-the-blank and meaning-in-context. Correct words go on the Word Wall in the Log. A Captain's Log prompt then asks students to use three of them in a short paragraph.
  • Illustrated scenes and sound: every passage has an animated scene. Sound effects are synthesised in the browser and can be muted with the Sound button (handy in a quiet classroom).
  • Extension task: each student writes a brand-new passage or side character with two choices and swaps it with a partner.
