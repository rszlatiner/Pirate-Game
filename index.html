🧑‍🏫 For teachers: using this in a creative writing class
  • Branching narrative: have students map the story on paper. Which choices lead to which endings? Where do paths merge?
  • Captain's Log prompts appear at five points (similes, dialogue, puns, monologue, voice). Students save their writing and copy their whole log into a document at the end.
  • Humour toolkit: look for exaggeration, deadpan, puns, running gags (the trousers, Kevin, the monocle) and rule-of-three.
  • Extension task: each student writes a brand-new passage with two choices and swaps it with a partner. Or rewrites an ending from another character's point of view.
  • Replay value: there are 8 endings. Challenge the class to find them all and compare which choices led where.
